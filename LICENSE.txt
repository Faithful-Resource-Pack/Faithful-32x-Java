We (Faithful) encourage using and modifying our work for your own projects. That
being said, there are a few ground rules to keep things fair for our artists and
developers. In general, you'll be fine if you're being honest about what you're
using and aren't trying to take advantage of our policies or contributors.

You may do the following:
1.  Use, modify, and distribute our work for both Minecraft and non-Minecraft

    resource packs, content creator edits, and your own games or software.
2.  Use our work as reference material or inspiration for your own projects.
3.  Use our work as placeholder content.
4.  Produce monetized videos or streams that use our packs.

Requirements if doing any of the above:
1.  Give clear credit to us in an appropriate and obvious place. This could be
    in a video or listing description, a project README or credits.txt file, or
    a resource pack's pack.mcmeta or manifest.json.
2.  Provide a clearly visible link to our website (https://faithfulpack.net).
3.  Be honest and clear about what you reference or use. When possible, don't
    just say that "some textures are from Faithful" with no further information;
    instead, try to cite the specific resource pack and content being used.
4.  Only use what you need to use. If you're making a resource pack that's
    mostly unmodified Faithful textures, consider making it an add-on that gets
    overlaid on top of a base pack instead.
5.  Either include an unmodified copy of this file or a link to our license at
    https://faithfulpack.net/license with all project distributions.

You may not do the following:
1.  Lock content using or modifying our work behind a paywall. This includes the
    Minecraft Marketplace and platforms like Patreon or Ko-fi.
2.  Use monetized download links or link shorteners. You can use reward systems
    offered by listing websites like CurseForge and Modrinth if your content is
    mostly your own, but avoid these if it's mostly unoriginal or simple edits.
3.  Indirectly take donations for content directly inspired by or based on our
    work. If Faithful-related content is one small aspect of a larger creation
    portfolio from which you receive donations, it's probably fine, but don't

4.  Use our work as a substitute for Minecraft's graphics when default textures
    otherwise wouldn't be allowed. It makes us look bad when Faithful is only
    seen as a free-to-use project that looks fairly similar to Minecraft's art,

5.  Make your projects look official when they aren't. Don't copy our branding
    or misleadingly call a project "official" in a way that would confuse an
    average user into believing we officially run or endorse it.
6.  Use our work in cryptocurrency or NFT-based projects.
7.  Train neural networks or AI models on our work.
